## What Changed

What was added, edited, or removed in the spec: sections, acceptance criteria, contracts, or scope. Be specific (e.g. "Added AC-7 for retry backoff; reworded AC-3; removed the deprecated bulk-export scope").

## Why

The product knowledge from the delivery that motivated the change: the behavior, contract, or constraint the session proved durable. Reference the observable evidence in product terms, not framework artifacts.

## How

Briefly, the shape of the change: how the spec now expresses the behavior (new criterion, tightened boundary, new spec package, etc.).
